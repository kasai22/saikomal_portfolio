How I use AI in engineering

Claude · ChatGPT

AI tools accelerate implementation. I remain responsible for understanding the code, assessing the output, integrating the change and validating that it works.

  1. 01 · FrameI write the requirements, constraints and acceptance criteria first, so the prompt describes a product decision rather than a guess.
  2. 02 · GenerateClaude and ChatGPT accelerate implementation — scaffolding, boilerplate, and first drafts of components and queries.
  3. 03 · ReviewI read and understand what comes back, adapt it to the codebase's conventions, and reject what does not hold up.
  4. 04 · ValidateI integrate the change, debug it, and verify it works — in the browser, against real data, and in the production build.
