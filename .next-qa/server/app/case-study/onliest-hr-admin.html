Enterprise HRMS

Onliest HR Admin

Onliest HR Admin is an internal HRMS. I designed role-based workflows for attendance, leave, onboarding and approvals across Admin, Manager and Employee roles — and the exception handling, bulk actions, audit trails and accessible table and form experiences that enterprise HR work depends on.

Project summary
Roles

Three roles, one set of records

Admin, Manager and Employee act on the same attendance, leave, onboarding and approval records with different responsibilities — so each role gets an interface shaped to its part of the work.

  • Role-based workflows for Admin, Manager and Employee users.
  • Attendance, leave, onboarding and approvals designed as connected workflows rather than separate screens.
  • Reusable Figma patterns shared with Onliest's Marketplace and Revenue products.
Patterns

Designed for the exceptions

Enterprise HR work is mostly the cases that do not follow the happy path. These are the patterns designed for them.

Exception handling

A clear path for the records that do not fit the rule, instead of a dead end.

Bulk actions

Acting on many records at once, for the roles that review work in volume.

Audit trails

A visible record of who changed what, and when, on every decision.

Accessible tables

Data-dense tables that stay readable and navigable by keyboard.

Accessible forms

Data-entry patterns with clear labels, validation and error recovery.
